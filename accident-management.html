Accident management · Hackbridge, South London

Someone hit your car. You should not have to run the claim as well.

Accident management means you make one call and then stop chasing. We take the details once, put the claim in the hands of our regulated claims partner, get the car recovered if it will not drive, and repair it here in our own bodyshop in Hackbridge — not at a third site you never see. You stay with us the whole way through, and you choose us as the repairer, which is your right and not your insurer’s decision.

What we actually do for you

You tell it once

Registration, what happened, the other driver’s details if you have them. We take it down once and work from that, instead of you repeating the story to four different people over a fortnight.

The claim goes to the regulated firm

The claim itself and the funding behind it are handled by our claims partner, who is the regulated company in the arrangement. We are the repairer and your point of contact. We will not pretend to be your solicitor or your insurer.

We get the car off the road

If it will not drive we recover it to the workshop rather than leaving it where it is while the claim gets going. Recovery runs from £120 + VAT up to 3 miles; beyond 10 miles we price it on the phone.

The repair happens here

The estimate is written here, the insurer’s engineer authorises it here, and the car is painted in our own booth here. Nothing is subcontracted to a site you cannot visit, and no stage falls between two businesses.

A vehicle while yours is in

On a non-fault claim a replacement may be arranged through our partner, who decides what you are entitled to on the facts. We also keep a small courtesy fleet of our own at £30 + VAT a day. We would rather quote a real figure than advertise a free car.

You get told what is happening

If a part is delayed or more damage appears once the bumper is off, you hear it from us when we find out, with photographs, not when you ring to ask why the promised date has gone past.

You choose the repairer. Not your insurer.

This is the part most people do not know, and insurers are not in a hurry to explain it. When you report a claim you will be offered a repairer from the insurer’s own approved network, and it is usually put to you as though it were the only option. It is not. The car is yours and where it gets repaired is your decision.

What does change is worth knowing before you decide, so here it is plainly. Insurers normally guarantee work carried out inside their own network, and some policies make certain benefits — a hire car, for instance — conditional on using it. Some will also ask you to cover a shortfall if our figure and their estimate differ. None of that stops you choosing us, but you should know it rather than discover it later. Send us your policy wording and we will go through it with you before you commit to anything.

What to do after a non-fault accident

What we are, and what we are not

Not an approved repairer

We are on no insurer’s approved-repairer panel and we are not BS10125 accredited. We would rather you heard that here than from your insurer. We work with insurers on the basis of your right to choose, which is a different thing and a stronger one.

Not the regulated claims firm

Our partner is. We are a repairer offering accident management as a service, and we do not advise you on your policy or on liability. If something needs a regulated answer, it comes from them, not from us.

Not a dealer price

Our bodywork labour runs around 40% below a main dealer rate. That is the figure we can stand behind; you will see bigger numbers advertised elsewhere and they are usually comparing against something convenient.

No deposit

Bodywork takes no deposit. You pay on collection, and Payment Assist is there if you want to spread it. The written quote is what the invoice is built from.

Put the registration in and we will come back to you

If the car is driveable, the quickest start is the registration and a photograph of the damage. If it is not driveable, call us — recovery is the first thing to sort out, not the paperwork.

Questions people actually ask

Am I allowed to choose my own repairer?

Yes. The car is yours and so is the decision. Insurers keep their own approved networks and will steer you towards one, but they cannot make you use it. Tell them you are using KITH Cars and we deal with them directly. What does change is the small print: insurers usually guarantee work carried out inside their own network, and some policies ask you to cover a shortfall if our figure and theirs differ. Send us your policy wording and we will go through it with you before you commit to anything.

Are you an insurer's approved repairer?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. We are not on any insurer's approved-repairer panel and we are not BS10125 accredited. We work with insurers on the basis that you have the right to choose your own repairer, which is a different thing, and we would rather tell you that up front than have you find out from your insurer.

Who actually handles the claim?

The claim and the funding behind it are handled by our claims partner, who is the regulated firm in the arrangement. KITH is the repairer and your point of contact: you deal with us, the car is repaired here, and we keep you updated. We are not a regulated claims company ourselves and we do not give advice about your policy.

Will I have to pay an excess?

It depends on how the claim is run and whether the other side accepts liability. On a non-fault claim handled through our partner there is often no excess for you to find, but that is not something anyone can promise before the facts are known. We will tell you which it is before any work starts, rather than after.

Will I get a replacement vehicle?

On a non-fault claim that may be arranged through our partner, who decides what you are entitled to based on the facts of the claim - so nobody here can promise you a car before that is assessed. Separately, we keep a small courtesy fleet of our own at £30 + VAT a day, £36 all in. We would rather quote a real figure than advertise a free car and recover the cost somewhere else in the bill.

Will a non-fault claim affect my no-claims discount?

If the claim is settled as non-fault and the costs are recovered from the other driver's insurer, your no-claims discount should not be affected. The risk is a claim that stays recorded as unsettled or ends up split. Your insurer is the one who decides how it is recorded, not us.

Can you recover the car if it is not driveable?

Yes. Recovery is priced by road miles from the workshop: up to 3 miles £120 + VAT, 3-5 miles £160, 5-8 miles £200, 8-10 miles £220, and anything beyond 10 miles is priced on the phone. On a non-fault claim recovery is usually part of what is recovered from the other side. Recovery is a transport job rather than repair work, so it carries no workmanship warranty.

How long does the repair take?

Two days to three weeks, depending on the damage and the parts. Wherever we can we book the car in once the parts are on the shelf, so it is not standing in pieces waiting for a back order. If a part is delayed part-way through we tell you as soon as we know instead of letting the date quietly drift.

What does the warranty cover?

Twelve months on our bodywork and paint: the paint adhering, the quality of the finish, the panel fit and the parts we supplied. It does not cover fresh stone chips, a new kerb scuff or a later incident. If something lifts, blisters or stops sitting right, bring it back - comebacks are not charged, they just need booking in.

What should I do at the scene of the accident?

Get the other driver's name, address, registration and insurer, photograph both cars where they came to rest and the wider scene, and take the details of anyone who saw it. Report it to your own insurer even if you do not intend to claim, because most policies require it. Then call us and we will take it from there.
