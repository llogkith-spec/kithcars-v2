How we handle scratch repair at Hackbridge

Assessment happens under strong, colour-corrected light with the panel clean and dry. The gauge readings matter because clear coat cannot be put back once it has been cut away. A factory panel normally has enough thickness to polish carefully; a panel somebody has already repaired can be thin, uneven or carrying old polish marks, and working that again risks burning through to colour. Where the readings say a panel will not take it, we tell you and quote to refinish instead, because a burn-through turns a modest job into a considerably larger one.

Colour is the part that catches people out. A paint code is not a single colour: manufacturers issue variants under the same code, and the paint on a ten-year-old car has moved anyway under sunlight, washing and polish. Getting the variant right is the difference between a repair you cannot find and one that is visible from across the road. Where a repair sits out in the middle of a large panel, the colour is carried into the surrounding area and faded away rather than stopped at a hard edge, because a hard edge is the first thing the eye picks up.

Pricing is by panel and by method, not by the number of scratches, which is why a long key mark down one side gets quoted area by area with the options set out separately. We will also tell you when not to spend: some marks come out in an hour, and some are better left until you are having other work done anyway. Fresh paint carries on curing after you collect the car, so leave waxing and machine polishing for about a month and keep a pressure washer away from the blended edge. The work carries our 12-month workmanship warranty, and we collect and deliver locally.

The cars we see

Dark solid and metallic colours show every swirl and wash mark, so most of the polishing work we do is on black, dark grey and deep blue cars, while silver hides a great deal. Three-layer pearl whites and reds are the hardest to match in a small area and usually need a wider blend. Matt and satin finishes cannot be polished at all and have to be refinished. Plastic bumpers and textured trim behave differently again to a steel wing, and leased cars arrive a few weeks before return carrying a season of marks.

Scratch Repair: what’s involved

Car paint is built up in layers: primer on the metal or plastic, a thin basecoat that gives the colour, and a clear coat on top that provides the gloss and protection. The clear coat is only hundredths of a millimetre thick, so how a scratch is repaired depends on which layer it has reached. Light swirls and marks that sit only in the clear coat can usually be machine polished out with no paint needed.

If your fingernail catches in the scratch, it has usually gone through the clear coat into the colour, or down to primer or bare metal. These need refinishing: the area is flatted, filled if needed, primed, painted in colour matched from your paint code and blended, then clear coated. Key scratches along one side often cross several panels, so we price each area honestly and explain where a smaller localised repair will do.

Types of scratch and what they need

Swirl marks and light hazing

Usually from automatic car washes or dirty wash mitts. These sit in the clear coat and normally polish out.

Fine scratches you can't feel

If your fingernail glides over it, the scratch is usually in the clear coat only and can be polished.

Scratches that catch a fingernail

These have usually gone into the colour or deeper, and need filling and paint rather than polish.

White or grey line in the scratch

Often primer showing through, which means the colour coat has been cut and the area needs refinishing.

Bare metal showing

Shiny metal or orange rust spots in the scratch. This needs repairing soon, before rust spreads under the paint.

Key scratches

Deliberate scratches are often deep and run across several panels, each of which needs refinishing and blending.

Stone chips on the bonnet and front

Small chips down to primer or metal. Depending on how many there are, they can be touched in or the panel refinished.

Scratches on bumpers and plastic trim

Painted plastic needs flexible preparation. Unpainted textured trim can't be polished in the same way and may need a specialist treatment or replacement.

When should it be done?

Repair scratches through to bare metal promptly, ideally within a few weeks, because exposed steel rusts and the rust spreads under the surrounding paint. Scratches in the clear coat only are cosmetic and can be polished out whenever it suits you. If the car has been keyed, report it to the police and check whether your policy covers vandalism before deciding whether to pay privately. Many owners also have scratches repaired before selling or returning a lease car.

  • Bare metal showing: as soon as practical, before rust starts
  • Scratches that catch a fingernail: within weeks, before the edges start to lift
  • Swirls and light marks: whenever convenient, often before a sale
  • Keyed panels: once the police and, if claiming, your insurer have been told
  • Before a lease or PCP return inspection

If it’s left

Clear coat scratches are cosmetic and won't spread. Scratches to bare steel will rust, and once rust creeps under the paint around the scratch, the repair area and the cost both grow. Plastic bumpers don't rust, but the edges of damaged paint can lift and flake over time.

Worth knowing

What people get wrong about scratch repair

Looking for the paint code on the V5C

It is not there. The code lives on a sticker on the car itself, usually in a door shut, under the bonnet or in the boot, and on some makes it needs a variant letter alongside it to be any use.

Going at it by hand with an abrasive

Household compounds and hard rubbing cut unevenly and take most material off the edges and body lines, which is precisely where clear coat is thinnest. The scratch fades and a dull patch takes its place.

Having the same panel polished every year

Every machine polish removes a little clear coat. A panel corrected three or four times may have nothing left to give, and at that point it needs paint rather than polish.

Washing with one bucket and a sponge

Most of the fine scratching we are asked to remove was put there at the wash. Two buckets, a proper mitt and rinsing it between panels prevents far more damage than any wax undoes.

Scratch Repair: your questions answered

Can you price it from a photograph?

We can give you a rough idea, but not a final figure. A photograph will not show how deep the scratch has gone or what the paint thickness is doing around it, and those two things decide the method and therefore the price. Bringing the car in takes a few minutes and means the figure you are given is the figure you pay.

Do I need to leave the car, or can I wait?

Polishing work can often be done while you wait, or inside a couple of hours. Anything involving paint needs the car for the day at least, because the repair has to be prepared, sprayed and properly cured before it goes back out into traffic. We tell you which applies when we quote, and we can collect and deliver locally instead.

What if the panel has been repainted before?

We will tell you before starting what that changes. Previous repairs can be thin, can have poor adhesion underneath, or can have been done in a slightly different shade, and any of those limits what we can promise you. Our 12-month workmanship warranty covers our own work, and we are straight about anything underneath it that we did not do.

Is it worth repairing before I sell or hand back a lease car?

Usually yes for anything a buyer or an inspector will notice, since leasing companies assess damage against a fair wear and tear standard with set charges attached. Get the written quote, compare it with what you expect to be charged or knocked down on the price, and decide from there. For one small mark, it is sometimes cheaper to leave it alone.

Can scratches be polished out?

If they're only in the clear coat, yes. A useful test is to run a fingernail across it: if it doesn't catch, polishing will usually remove it. Polishing takes off a small amount of clear coat, so we avoid aggressive or repeated polishing of the same area.

Are touch-up pens any good?

Touch-up paint is useful for sealing small stone chips so the metal doesn't rust, but it won't give an invisible finish on a line scratch. We can neatly touch in chips, or refinish the area properly if you want it to disappear.

Will I need the whole panel painted?

Not always. A short scratch near the edge or corner of a panel can often be done as a localised SMART repair. Long scratches, or ones in the middle of a large panel like a bonnet, usually need the panel refinished and blended into the next.

Can you match metallic and pearl paints?

Yes. The colour is matched from your paint code and blended into adjacent panels. Metallic and pearl paints change appearance with viewing angle, which is exactly why blending is used rather than painting one panel edge to edge.

Is scratch repair covered by insurance?

Keying is vandalism and is usually covered by comprehensive policies, subject to your excess and possibly your no-claims discount. For one or two panels, paying privately is often cheaper overall, and our written quote helps you compare.

Can I choose my own repairer, or do I have to use the one my insurer names?

You choose. Insurers maintain their own approved networks and will steer you towards them, but the car is yours and so is the decision. Tell your insurer you are using KITH Cars and we will deal with them directly. Be aware of what changes: an insurer typically guarantees work done inside its own network, and outside it some policies ask you to cover a shortfall if our figure and theirs differ. Read your own policy wording, or send it to us and we will go through it with you before you commit to anything.

Is there a courtesy car while mine is in?

Yes, subject to availability, at 30 pounds plus VAT a day - 36 pounds a day all in. We would rather quote a real figure than advertise a free car and recover it somewhere else in the bill. Ask when you book and we will tell you straight away whether one is free for your dates.
