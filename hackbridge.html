Hackbridge drivers

What we see on cars from Hackbridge

Almost everything that comes to us from Hackbridge itself arrived under its own steam from a few streets away, and it shows in what the damage looks like. It is parked damage, not road damage.

Sutton Council’s ward parking survey for Wandle ward, which is Hackbridge, found 67 per cent of residents saying their street has a parking problem, 39 per cent parking on the road, and 74 per cent saying the trouble is on weekdays. On top of that, the old Felnex estate — about a million square feet of former industry — was approved in 2016 for 725 homes plus a supermarket, a surgery, a care home and a bus interchange, and New Mill Quarter adds another 440. Over eleven hundred new homes, going into a suburb whose central parade is Edwardian.

So: streets parked solid on both sides, years of contractor and delivery traffic squeezing through them, and roads that were drawn for a fraction of it. Scuffed bumper corners, creased doors, mirrors taken off by passing vans, kerbed alloys.

The mechanical side has its own local signature, and it comes from the station. Hackbridge is Zone 4 and about 25 minutes from Victoria, so a great many cars here exist to cover one mile twice a day. That is the hardest duty cycle you can give a modern car: diesel particulate filters that never get hot enough to clear, batteries that never fully recharge, exhausts that rot from the inside, brakes that corrode between uses. We see more of all four from Hackbridge than from anywhere else we cover.

Why being the garage in the village actually matters

Most garages describe themselves as local. We are on London Road, which for a good deal of Hackbridge is a walk rather than a drive, and that has practical consequences rather than sentimental ones.

  • You can bring it down and have it looked at. Not booked in for a fortnight on Thursday so somebody can hear you describe a noise.
  • You can leave it and walk home. No lift to arrange, no waiting room.
  • We can see it before we quote. Where a job needs the car on a ramp to price honestly, that is a short detour rather than a second trip across town.
  • We have to live with what we tell you. In a suburb this size, selling somebody four tyres when they needed two is the last work you get from them or anyone they know.

That last one is the real difference and it cuts against us as often as for us. We tell people their car is not worth repairing, that the dent they are worried about will cost more to fix than it takes off the value, and that a PDR specialist down the road will do a particular job cheaper than we will, because every one of those conversations happens again in the Co-op.

Finding us

We are on London Road, which is the point

There is no journey to describe. We are at Unit 1 Soho Mills on London Road, which is the road you are already on — a couple of minutes from the station, the parade and the level crossing, and inside the SM6 postcode rather than a drive away from it.

That changes how you can use us, and it is worth saying plainly:

  • You can walk in. If something is making a noise, bring it down and we will look at it rather than booking you in for a week on Thursday to hear a description of it.
  • You can leave the car and walk home. Most of Hackbridge is ten minutes on foot from the yard, which removes the whole business of arranging a lift or waiting in a reception.
  • You can get to the station from us. If the car has to stay in, you are a few minutes from the platform rather than stranded.
  • We can look at it before quoting. Where a job needs the car on a ramp to price honestly, being local means that is a short detour rather than a second trip across town.

Being the workshop in the place rather than the workshop near it is most of why people use us. It is also why we would rather tell you something is not worth repairing than take the work — we are going to see you in the Co-op.

Questions

Hackbridge: your questions answered

Where are you exactly?

Unit 1, Soho Mills, London Road, Hackbridge SM6 7HN — a couple of minutes from Hackbridge station and the parade, with room to leave a car.

Can I walk in without booking?

To have something looked at, yes. For work that needs a ramp and a slot, call or put your registration into the box on this page first so we can give you a time.

Do you do both mechanical work and bodywork?

Both, on the same site. Garage and bodyshop, so a car that needs paint and a clutch does not have to go to two places.

Are you on any insurer panel?

No, and we are on no manufacturer scheme either. On a claim the repairer is your choice, and nothing we tell you is steered by an arrangement with an insurer.

Do you do paintless dent removal?

No. Every dent we repair is straightened and then painted and blended. On the dents PDR can fix, a specialist will be cheaper and we would rather tell you that.

My car barely does any miles. Does it still need servicing?

Usually yes, on the time interval rather than the mileage one. Short local journeys are harder on oil, brakes and batteries than the odometer suggests, which is the single most common thing we explain to people in Hackbridge.
