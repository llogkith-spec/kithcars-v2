Published 2026 figures put independent workshops at roughly £55 to £95 an hour, mainstream franchised dealers at about £85 to £165, and premium marques higher again. We sit at £84.

Labour is the larger part of most bodywork bills, because panel repair is mostly hours rather than parts. On a repair rather than a replacement, there may be no significant part at all — which is exactly where the rate difference tells.

What you are not giving up

  • Your warranty. Servicing and repair at an independent, to the manufacturer’s schedule with the right specification of parts, keeps a manufacturer warranty intact. That is the legal position.
  • Your insurer. On any claim the repairer is your choice. We are on no insurer panel, which means no labour time has been pre-agreed with anybody over your head.
  • Paint quality. Colour matched to the car with a spray-out card and blended into the neighbouring panels, with twelve months on it in writing.

Where the dealer is genuinely the better answer

There are real ones, and a page that pretended otherwise would not be worth reading.

We will say so when one of these applies rather than having a go at your expense.

What we are not

We are on no insurer panel and no manufacturer scheme. Nothing on this site says we hold an approval we do not hold, and a bodyshop that implies one it has not got is telling you something about how it handles the rest of the job.

What that costs us is the work insurers send to their own panels. What it buys you is that nothing we recommend is steered by an arrangement with one.

Whoever you claim through, the repairer is your choice. KITH is on no insurer’s panel, which means no labour time has been pre-agreed on your car’s behalf — we estimate the job as it needs doing and argue it with the engineer if we have to.

How to compare two quotes properly

  1. Ask whether blending is included. The commonest hidden difference between two bodywork quotes, and the reason one repair shows in daylight.
  2. Ask what the paint warranty is, in writing. Ours is twelve months.
  3. Ask whether sensor recalibration is in the price. On a modern bumper it may be needed, and it should be visible rather than a surprise at collection.
  4. Ask what happens if the strip-down finds more. You want to be told before the work happens, not after.
  5. Compare the hours, not just the total. Two quotes at the same price can be very different amounts of work.

Your questions answered

How much cheaper are you than a main dealer?

On bodywork labour, around 42%. Our body rate is £84 an hour including VAT against a main dealer rate of £144. Labour is the larger part of most bodywork bills.

Will using you void my warranty?

No. Work done to the manufacturer’s schedule, with parts of the right specification and a proper record, keeps a manufacturer warranty intact.

Does my insurer have to agree?

The repairer is your choice on any claim. Your insurer may suggest its own panel; it cannot require you to use one.

Are you cheaper on everything?

On labour, consistently. On parts it depends what the car needs — some manufacturer parts have one source and cost what they cost, and we will tell you when that is the case.

When should I go to the dealer instead?

An open recall, a warranty repair they are paying for, a structural job with a marque-specific method, or coding only the network can do. We will say so.
