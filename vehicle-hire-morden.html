About Morden

Morden is a suburban town at the southern end of the Northern line, and grew rapidly after Morden Underground station opened in 1926, with its population rising from about 1,300 in 1921 to over 12,000 by 1931. Much of the housing is 1920s and 1930s semis and terraces, including a large part of the London County Council's St Helier estate, laid out along wide avenues and cul-de-sacs. The town centre is compact and dominated by the station and the Crown House civic tower, with generous parks on either side. It is a family and commuter suburb where most journeys are by car or bus to the station, so the few main roads carry most of the traffic.

Questions

Vehicle Hire in Morden: your questions answered

I commute from Morden by Tube. Is it worth hiring while my car is repaired?

It depends on what else you use the car for. If it is only occasional, you may manage without; if you rely on it for school runs, shopping or visiting family, daily hire from £30 keeps things going. We will give you a realistic repair timescale so you can decide.

Can I park the hire car in my Morden resident's bay?

Not automatically, as permits are usually tied to your own vehicle. Check with Merton Council how to cover a temporary vehicle for your zone before you park it at home.

What speed limit applies on St Helier Avenue now?

It was cut to 30mph, with 20mph at the Rose Hill roundabout. Any fines during the hire are the hirer's responsibility, so it is worth keeping an eye on the limit in an unfamiliar car.

What do I need to hire a vehicle?

Typically a full valid driving licence, proof of identity and address, a deposit and suitable insurance cover. There may also be minimum age and licence-held requirements. Ask us for the exact terms when you book, as they can vary by vehicle.

Is a courtesy car the same as hire?

No. Courtesy cars are offered subject to availability. Hire is a separate daily-rate service, useful for longer repairs or when no courtesy car is free. We will tell you which options are available for your job.

Is insurance included?

Ask us when you book. Depending on the arrangement, cover may be provided with the hire or you may need your own, and there is typically an excess or deposit. We explain the terms before you sign anything.

Can I hire from you after an accident?

If your insurer, or the other driver's insurer, provides a replacement vehicle, that is usually the first option to check. If you are paying yourself or cover is not available, you can hire from us while your car is repaired at our bodyshop.

What if my repair takes longer than planned?

We call you as soon as we know, and you can extend the hire. You pay for the days you have the vehicle, so we aim to give accurate timescales from the start.
