How our bodywork pricing works

Three things decide a bodywork price: which panel, how bad the damage is, and how big the car is. Nothing else — there is no surcharge on our rate card for metallic, pearl, aluminium or a colour change.

Every figure is shown as a band, and the band runs upward from the card price rather than either side of it. The card figure is the floor. What we cannot know before seeing the car is how much more the job is, never how much less.

Blending the paint into the neighbouring panels is included in every figure. It is the first thing cut from a cheap quote and the main reason a repair shows in daylight, so we do not price it separately or leave it out.

Your questions answered

Can I get a price without bringing the car in?

Yes. Put your registration into the box above and pick the panel and the damage. You will get a band immediately, and we confirm the exact figure when we see it.

Do you do paintless dent removal?

No. Every dent we repair is straightened and then painted and blended. On the dents PDR can fix, a PDR specialist will be cheaper, and we would rather say so.

Is blending really included?

Yes, on every figure. Ask anyone else quoting you the same question — the answers will tell you more than the prices.

How long will it take?

A localised repair is often same or next day. A panel off, painted and refitted is two to three days, because paint has to cure.
