Sutton drivers

What we see on cars from Sutton

Sutton's High Street is pedestrianised for the best part of a mile, so the driving happens around it. The one-way system on Throwley Way and St Nicholas Way keeps traffic circulating, and at peak times and on Saturdays it backs up with queues for the car parks serving the St Nicholas Centre and Times Square. Car park damage is one of the most common reasons a Sutton car arrives at our bodyshop: scraped door skins from tight bays, corner scuffs from concrete pillars, kerbed alloys on the ramps, and the anonymous dent that was there when you came back with the shopping. Most of those are straightened and repainted for well under the cost of a new panel.

Residential Sutton adds its own. The streets around the town centre are tightly parked, the Sutton Town Centre Controlled Parking Zone pushes all-day parkers out into the surrounding roads, and school-run traffic is heavy given the number of schools here. That mix produces low-speed knocks, kerbed wheels and mirror damage rather than serious accident work, though we handle that too. We photograph the damage, quote in writing, and explain where a smart repair and a blend into the adjacent panel will look right and where a proper panel repair is the honest answer, particularly on the metallic and pearl finishes common on newer cars.

Mechanically, Sutton's traffic is a story of heat and stop-start. The A232 through the town has a long run of signals and bus stops, the by-pass junctions queue at peak times, and air conditioning, clutches and cooling systems all feel that. Weak air conditioning is the classic summer complaint on a car that spends its life crawling, and we regas both R134a and R1234yf systems after a leak check rather than simply topping up and sending you away. Clutch and gearbox work, diagnostics and MOTs all happen here too, with the bodyshop next door if the car needs both.

Around Sutton

  • Sutton stationThe borough's main interchange, with fast trains reaching London Victoria in about twenty-five minutes, so the streets nearby fill with all-day commuter parking.
  • St Nicholas CentreThe three-level shopping centre off the High Street; its car park traffic is a large part of why the one-way system queues on Saturdays.
  • Times SquareThe other town-centre shopping centre, opened in 1985 and refitted in 2017, with the same tight bays and busy access roads.
  • Manor ParkThe town-centre park holding Sutton's 1921 war memorial, a useful landmark on the walk between the High Street and the surrounding parking.
  • Royal Marsden HospitalThe specialist cancer hospital at the southern end of town, next to the Institute of Cancer Research, and a steady generator of staff and visitor traffic.
  • Sutton United, Gander Green LaneThe town's football club plays at Gander Green Lane, well away from the High Street and the one-way system that surrounds it.

Questions

Sutton: your questions answered

Can you collect the car while I am at work in Sutton?

Yes, collection and delivery is available locally and Sutton is a short run for us. It is a practical option if you are leaving the keys before a shift at one of the hospitals or an office in the centre, and the car comes back once the work is signed off and paid for.

Someone dented my car in a town-centre car park. What now?

Bring it in or send us photographs and we will quote in writing. Most car park dents can be straightened and the panel repainted for less than a new panel costs. Where the paint is broken or the crease is sharp, we will tell you that a filled and painted repair is the proper fix.

Is it quicker to come to you than to drive around the town centre?

For most of Sutton, yes. If you start in the centre you will use Throwley Way or St Nicholas Way to reach the A232, but only briefly, and from Carshalton Road onwards it is signals rather than gyratories. It also keeps you away from the Saturday queues for the shopping centre car parks.

Can you do an MOT and any repairs the same day?

Often, if the parts are available and you book the MOT early in the day. If something fails, we will call you with the details and a price before doing anything, and you decide whether the work goes ahead here or elsewhere.
