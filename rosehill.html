Rosehill drivers

What we see on cars from Rosehill

The six-way roundabout is the centre of everything in Rosehill, and it generates a particular kind of damage. Lane changing between the A217, the A297 and Wrythe Lane, buses holding a line and traffic filtering in from the parades at Rosehill Court all add up to low-speed contact: a scuffed front corner, a creased wing, a rear bumper pushed in by the car behind. We take both insurance and self-pay accident work, and the paint is mixed and sprayed here, so a repaired panel is matched to the car in front of us rather than approximately to a factory code.

Plenty of cars on the St Helier estate side have been on the road a long time and are looked after accordingly, kept running because they do what their owners need, and that changes what an MOT looks like. On a car past ten or twelve years old the failures are rarely dramatic engine faults; they are corroded brake pipes, rusted sills and suspension mounting points, perished bushes, a sagging exhaust and lights that have filled with water. We can weld and repair a corroded section where it is safe and sensible to do so, rather than treating rust as the end of a car that is otherwise sound. Where it is not sensible, we say so plainly instead of quoting for work that costs more than the car.

Being close by changes how a repair fits into your week. Wrythe Lane is busy all day with hospital traffic, and shift patterns mean cars get used at odd hours, so an 8am to 8pm Monday to Friday and 8am to 5pm on Saturday workshop two and a half miles away is easier to build a day around than somewhere across the borough. If a car will not start on the estate we can recover it in, and for anything that runs but is not safe to drive, leave it where it is and let us fetch it.

Questions

Rosehill: your questions answered

Is a courtesy car available while mine is in?

Sometimes, and it is always subject to availability rather than guaranteed, so ask when you book and we will tell you honestly. Given that we are only two and a half miles from the Rose Hill roundabout, collection and delivery, or a lift back, often works out simpler anyway.

Can you collect from the St Helier estate?

Yes. Collection and delivery is available locally and the estate is on our doorstep. The cottage streets are narrow and parked on both sides, so it helps if you tell us roughly where the car is and whether there is space to get alongside it.

My car is old. Is it worth repairing for the MOT?

That depends on what has failed and what the car is worth to you, and it is your decision to make with the numbers in front of you. We measure and photograph what we find, quote it in writing, and separate the work that makes a car safe and legal from the work that is merely tidy.

Is it worth coming back for the car the same day?

An MOT, a tyre change or a battery is quick enough that one trip covers it. Welding, paint and clutch work are not, so with a journey this short most people drop the car in the morning and return for it later in the day, up to our 8pm closing. Ask when you book what the likely timings are and you can plan around them.
