About Wimbledon

Wimbledon is an affluent south-west London suburb with two distinct centres: Wimbledon Village up on the hill by the Common, with its older houses, boutiques and restaurants, and the busier town centre down Wimbledon Hill Road around the station, The Broadway and the Centre Court Shopping Centre. Housing ranges from large Victorian and Edwardian houses in the Village and around the Common to terraces and modern flats towards South Wimbledon and Raynes Park. It is world-famous for The Championships at the All England Club, which transform local traffic for a fortnight each summer. Driving means steep hills, a busy town centre, bus-heavy main roads and extensive controlled parking.

Places nearby: All England Lawn Tennis and Croquet Club, Wimbledon Common, Wimbledon Windmill, Cannizaro Park, Wimbledon Park, Centre Court Shopping Centre

Questions

Vehicle Hire in Wimbledon: your questions answered

Can I hire a premium or electric car while my Wimbledon SUV is repaired?

Our hire cars are everyday cars for commuting and family use. Ask us when you book what is available, and we will tell you honestly if it does not match what you need.

Does my Merton permit cover the hire car in Wimbledon?

Not automatically, as permits are usually tied to a registered vehicle. Check with Merton Council how to cover a temporary vehicle before parking the hire car in your zone.

Can I use the hire car during The Championships?

Yes, but roads around the All England Club close to most traffic during the tournament. Plan your routes, and remember that any charges during the hire are usually the hirer's responsibility.

What do I need to hire a vehicle?

Typically a full valid driving licence, proof of identity and address, a deposit and suitable insurance cover. There may also be minimum age and licence-held requirements. Ask us for the exact terms when you book, as they can vary by vehicle.

Is a courtesy car the same as hire?

No. Courtesy cars are offered subject to availability. Hire is a separate daily-rate service, useful for longer repairs or when no courtesy car is free. We will tell you which options are available for your job.

Is insurance included?

Ask us when you book. Depending on the arrangement, cover may be provided with the hire or you may need your own, and there is typically an excess or deposit. We explain the terms before you sign anything.

Can I hire from you after an accident?

If your insurer, or the other driver's insurer, provides a replacement vehicle, that is usually the first option to check. If you are paying yourself or cover is not available, you can hire from us while your car is repaired at our bodyshop.

What if my repair takes longer than planned?

We call you as soon as we know, and you can extend the hire. You pay for the days you have the vehicle, so we aim to give accurate timescales from the start.
