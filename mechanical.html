An independent garage is defined by how much it keeps in-house, and we keep a lot. Servicing and MOT preparation share the same ramps as cambelts, timing chains, head gaskets, turbochargers and gearbox work, so a rattle at 2,000rpm does not have to be passed between three premises before anybody commits to an answer. Diagnostics is treated as a job in its own right rather than ten free minutes with a code reader, because a stored code names a circuit, not a culprit. Certain makes come through constantly, among them Audi, BMW, Alfa Romeo, Citroën and Fiat, and knowing where those engines habitually fail shortens the search considerably. Fitting parts in sequence and hoping the symptom disappears is not diagnosis, and it is not how a bill gets built here.

Every job opens with the registration, which tells us the exact engine, build date and specification. The right oil approval, filter and friction material are then on the shelf before you arrive, rather than being ordered while your car occupies a ramp. At check-in the mileage goes on the record and your description of the fault is written down in your own words, because the conditions a problem shows up under are usually more useful than the noise itself. From there the order never changes: confirm the complaint, measure or test rather than assume, then replace. You are telephoned with the findings before anything is committed to. The vehicle is road tested afterwards, and whatever came off stays on the bench until you have collected it.

Nothing begins without a written quote, and that quote names the parts instead of giving you a single number. Where there is a genuine choice between an OE part and an OE-equivalent from the firm that supplies the manufacturer, you are given both figures and the difference between them explained, rather than having the decision taken on your behalf. Diagnostic time is charged as time; where it leads straight into the repair, that charge comes off the bill. If something turns up that was not on the quote, the job stops and becomes a phone call, never an extra line noticed at the end. Servicing starts from £182, an MOT is £50 when the DVSA permits a station to charge up to £54.85, and every repair is backed for three months.

Questions

Common questions

Will using an independent garage affect my manufacturer warranty?

No, provided the work is done properly. Under Block Exemption rules a manufacturer cannot insist you use its own dealers to keep a new-car warranty, as long as the vehicle is serviced to the published schedule using parts of matching quality and the work is recorded. We service to the maker's schedule, fit OE or OE-equivalent parts and give you an invoice detailing the oil specification and parts used.

Can you carry out repairs after a failed MOT?

Yes, and most people do it that way because the car is already here. You get a written quote for the failure items before any work starts, and you are free to take the vehicle elsewhere instead. The retest rules are worth knowing before you decide. Leave the vehicle with us and have it retested within ten working days and the partial retest is free. Take it away and bring it back within ten working days and it is a partial retest at half the usual fee, unless the failure is one of a short list of items DVSA allows to be put right and returned by the end of the next working day, which is free.

How do I know which level of service my car needs?

It depends on mileage, age and what was done last time, not on a sales ladder. Give us the registration and roughly how many miles you cover a year and we will tell you which items are actually due, including anything on its own interval such as brake fluid, spark plugs or a cambelt. Paying for a major service when an interim is due does your engine no extra good.

What accreditations does the garage hold?

We are members of the Independent Garage Association and Trust My Garage, and we work to the TSI Approved Code, which is the Trading Standards-backed consumer code. In practice that means a written estimate, clear invoicing and an independent route for complaints. We are also proud to use Castrol oils.
