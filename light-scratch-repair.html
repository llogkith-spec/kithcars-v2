Is this what you have?

Four checks, in order. They take about two minutes in daylight and they decide which repair you are actually looking at.

  1. Run a fingernail gently across the scratch, at a right angle to it. Run a fingernail gently across the scratch, at a right angle to it.
  2. If it glides over, the damage is in the clear lacquer on top and the colour underneath is intact. If it glides over, the damage is in the clear lacquer on top and the colour underneath is intact.
  3. If it catches, it has gone through to the primer or the metal, and that is a different and dearer job. If it catches, it has gone through to the primer or the metal, and that is a different and dearer job.
  4. Wet the scratch with a drop of water. If it disappears while wet and returns as it dries, it is lacquer-deep.

How we repair it

The area is flatted back with progressively finer abrasive until the edges of the scratch are level with the surrounding lacquer, then the lacquer is rebuilt over it and machine polished until the gloss matches the panel around it.

The panel stays on the car and only the affected area is refinished, which is why this is the cheapest route out of any paint damage. There is no colour involved, so there is no colour to match.

What you can try yourself

A scratch remover from a motor factor is genuinely worth trying on lacquer damage, and it sometimes works. What it cannot do is add lacquer back, so it only helps where there is enough thickness left to flatten into. If two attempts have not shifted it, stop — the risk from here is going through the lacquer entirely and turning a cheap job into a panel respray.

When to stop. If two careful attempts have not improved it, stop there. Almost every job we see that cost more than it should have is one where a reasonable first attempt turned into a fourth.

How soon it needs doing

Lacquer damage is not urgent. Nothing is corroding, because the colour and primer below are untouched. It is worth doing before you sell the car and worth doing if it irritates you, but there is no clock running.

Your questions answered

Can you polish any scratch out?

No, and anyone who says yes is going to disappoint you. Polishing removes a little lacquer to level the surface. If the scratch is deeper than the lacquer, there is nothing to level into.

Will the polished area look different?

Not when it is done properly. The area is polished and then the whole panel is brought to the same finish, so there is no bright patch where the work was.

How long does it take?

Usually the same day, and often while you wait for a single scratch.

Is it worth claiming on insurance?

Almost never. This is well under any normal excess.
