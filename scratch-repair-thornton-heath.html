Many of those long marks are shallower than they look. We wash the panel, inspect it under strong light and run a fingernail across the scratch. If it glides over, the mark is in the clear coat, and a machine polish with a cutting compound and finishing polish will usually remove it. Scratch Repair: what’s involved

Car paint is built up in layers: primer on the metal or plastic, a thin basecoat that gives the colour, and a clear coat on top that provides the gloss and protection. The clear coat is only hundredths of a millimetre thick, so how a scratch is repaired depends on which layer it has reached. Light swirls and marks that sit only in the clear coat can usually be machine polished out with no paint needed.

If your fingernail catches in the scratch, it has usually gone through the clear coat into the colour, or down to primer or bare metal. These need refinishing: the area is flatted, filled if needed, primed, painted in colour matched from your paint code and blended, then clear coated. Key scratches along one side often cross several panels, so we price each area honestly and explain where a smaller localised repair will do.

Types of scratch and what they need

Swirl marks and light hazing

Usually from automatic car washes or dirty wash mitts. These sit in the clear coat and normally polish out.

Fine scratches you can't feel

If your fingernail glides over it, the scratch is usually in the clear coat only and can be polished.

Scratches that catch a fingernail

These have usually gone into the colour or deeper, and need filling and paint rather than polish.

White or grey line in the scratch

Often primer showing through, which means the colour coat has been cut and the area needs refinishing.

Bare metal showing

Shiny metal or orange rust spots in the scratch. This needs repairing soon, before rust spreads under the paint.

Key scratches

Deliberate scratches are often deep and run across several panels, each of which needs refinishing and blending.

Stone chips on the bonnet and front

Small chips down to primer or metal. Depending on how many there are, they can be touched in or the panel refinished.

Scratches on bumpers and plastic trim

Painted plastic needs flexible preparation. Unpainted textured trim can't be polished in the same way and may need a specialist treatment or replacement.

When should it be done?

Repair scratches through to bare metal promptly, ideally within a few weeks, because exposed steel rusts and the rust spreads under the surrounding paint. Scratches in the clear coat only are cosmetic and can be polished out whenever it suits you. If the car has been keyed, report it to the police and check whether your policy covers vandalism before deciding whether to pay privately. Many owners also have scratches repaired before selling or returning a lease car.

  • Bare metal showing: as soon as practical, before rust starts
  • Scratches that catch a fingernail: within weeks, before the edges start to lift
  • Swirls and light marks: whenever convenient, often before a sale
  • Keyed panels: once the police and, if claiming, your insurer have been told
  • Before a lease or PCP return inspection

Scratch Repair in Thornton Heath: your questions answered

A bus clipped my mirror on Brigstock Road. Can the cover be repaired?

If it's painted and not cracked, usually yes: it's removed, prepared and refinished in your paint colour. If the housing is broken or the cover is unpainted textured plastic, replacement may be the better option.

I drive for a living. How long will a scratch repair take?

Polishing is often done in an hour or two. Refinishing usually means leaving the car for a day. We'll give you an honest time with the written quote so you can plan your shifts.

Do you repair scratches on older cars at a sensible price?

Yes. We'll explain the options, from sealing a scratch to stop rust through to a full refinish, and quote for what makes sense for the car's age and value.

Can scratches be polished out?

If they're only in the clear coat, yes. A useful test is to run a fingernail across it: if it doesn't catch, polishing will usually remove it. Polishing takes off a small amount of clear coat, so we avoid aggressive or repeated polishing of the same area.

Are touch-up pens any good?

Touch-up paint is useful for sealing small stone chips so the metal doesn't rust, but it won't give an invisible finish on a line scratch. We can neatly touch in chips, or refinish the area properly if you want it to disappear.

Will I need the whole panel painted?

Not always. A short scratch near the edge or corner of a panel can often be done as a localised SMART repair. Long scratches, or ones in the middle of a large panel like a bonnet, usually need the panel refinished and blended into the next.

Can you match metallic and pearl paints?

Yes. The colour is matched from your paint code and blended into adjacent panels. Metallic and pearl paints change appearance with viewing angle, which is exactly why blending is used rather than painting one panel edge to edge.

Is scratch repair covered by insurance?

Keying is vandalism and is usually covered by comprehensive policies, subject to your excess and possibly your no-claims discount. For one or two panels, paying privately is often cheaper overall, and our written quote helps you compare.
