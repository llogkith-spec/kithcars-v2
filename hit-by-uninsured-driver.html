Two schemes, often confused

Uninsured driverUntraced driver
You know who hit youYesNo
SchemeUninsured Drivers AgreementUntraced Drivers Agreement
Vehicle damage coveredYesOnly with a significant injury
First thing to doGet their details and check the MIDHunt for evidence of who they were

People use “MIB claim” for both and the difference matters enormously. If you have a name and a registration, you are in the left-hand column and your car damage is recoverable.

At the scene, in order

  1. Get the details anyway. Name, address, registration, and the make and colour. Legally they must give you their name and address. Photograph the car and the plate yourself rather than relying on what you are told.
  2. Photograph everything. Both cars, the positions, the wider scene, the road layout. Do it before anything is moved.
  3. Get a witness if there is one. A name and number. Uninsured drivers dispute liability more often than insured ones, because they have more to lose.
  4. Report it to the police. Driving without insurance is an offence, and you want the report.
  5. Check the Motor Insurance Database. The askMID service tells you whether a registration was insured. It is the difference between suspecting and knowing.

Then pick your route

  • Through your own comprehensive insurer. Usually the fastest. They repair the car, then pursue the uninsured driver themselves. Ask explicitly whether your excess and no-claims will be restored once liability is established — they often are, but get the answer before you commit.
  • Through the MIB. The route if you are third-party only, or if your insurer will not pursue it. Vehicle damage is covered and an excess applies to it.
  • Directly against the driver. Possible, and often not worth it. Someone driving uninsured frequently has nothing to recover from.

Whoever you claim through, the repairer is your choice. KITH is on no insurer’s panel, which means no labour time has been pre-agreed on your car’s behalf — we estimate the job as it needs doing and argue it with the engineer if we have to.

The bit to watch on your own policy

An uninsured-driver claim can sit on your record as a fault claim while liability is being established, because your insurer has not recovered its money yet. That can affect a renewal that falls in the middle.

Ask your insurer to confirm in writing what happens to your no-claims and your excess if recovery succeeds, and keep that confirmation. It is the thing people wish they had eighteen months later.

Your questions answered

How do I check if a car is insured?

The askMID service checks a registration against the Motor Insurance Database. Do it the same day — it tells you which scheme you are in.

They gave me details but no insurance certificate.

That is common and not conclusive either way. Check the MID rather than taking their word, and do not accept a promise to sort it privately before you have.

Will my no-claims be affected?

Possibly while liability is being settled, and often restored once your insurer recovers. Get the answer in writing before you claim.

Can I use any bodyshop?

Yes. Whichever route you go down, choosing the repairer is yours. We are on no insurer panel, so there is no arrangement pushing you anywhere.

They have offered me cash to keep it quiet.

Your call, but price the repair properly first. Cash offers at the roadside are reliably below what the job costs, and once you have accepted it the route back is hard.
