About Cheam

Cheam is a leafy, mostly residential suburb on the western side of the London Borough of Sutton, split into Cheam Village, North Cheam and South Cheam. The village grew up around a crossroads and keeps a conservation-area feel, with Tudor and Georgian buildings alongside 1920s–30s mock-Tudor shopping parades, while the surrounding streets are largely inter-war semis and detached family houses. North Cheam, a mile to the north around the A24, is busier and more commercial. Driving is mostly on wide suburban roads, but the village crossroads and the main A-roads through it get congested at peak times.

Places nearby: Nonsuch Park, Nonsuch Mansion, Cheam Park, Whitehall, Lumley Chapel, St Dunstan's Church

Vehicle Hire in Cheam: your questions answered

I live near Cheam Village in the Park Lane CPZ. Can I park a hire car outside my house?

A hire car is not automatically covered by your permit. Check with Sutton Council how to cover a temporary vehicle, as any parking charges during the hire are usually the hirer's responsibility.

Can I hire an automatic while my Cheam family car is in for a gearbox repair?

Possibly. Automatic availability varies, so tell us when you book and we will check what is free for your dates.

How long will I need the hire car for?

That depends on the repair. We give you a realistic timescale when we quote, match the hire to it and call you if anything changes.

What do I need to hire a vehicle?

Typically a full valid driving licence, proof of identity and address, a deposit and suitable insurance cover. There may also be minimum age and licence-held requirements. Ask us for the exact terms when you book, as they can vary by vehicle.

Is a courtesy car the same as hire?

No. Courtesy cars are offered subject to availability. Hire is a separate daily-rate service, useful for longer repairs or when no courtesy car is free. We will tell you which options are available for your job.

Is insurance included?

Ask us when you book. Depending on the arrangement, cover may be provided with the hire or you may need your own, and there is typically an excess or deposit. We explain the terms before you sign anything.

Can I hire from you after an accident?

If your insurer, or the other driver's insurer, provides a replacement vehicle, that is usually the first option to check. If you are paying yourself or cover is not available, you can hire from us while your car is repaired at our bodyshop.

What if my repair takes longer than planned?

We call you as soon as we know, and you can extend the hire. You pay for the days you have the vehicle, so we aim to give accurate timescales from the start.
