Dents in Wimbledon, and where they come from

SW19 records 4,493 collisions, the highest of any district in Merton and more than any Sutton district. Wimbledon carries town-centre congestion, the A219 and A238, and two weeks a year when the traffic is unlike anywhere else in the country.

SW19 has 4,493 recorded collisions. Across the London Borough of Merton the total is 12,428, with 1,671 of them serious.

Wimbledon cars are on average newer, more expensive and more difficult to paint than the rest of our patch - more metallics, more pearls, more aluminium panels. That does not change the dent, but it changes the repair: matching and blending matter more, aluminium needs different preparation and isolation from steel tooling, and a three-stage pearl has to be sprayed out on a test card before it goes near the car.

Aluminium is the one that catches people out on newer Wimbledon cars. A growing number use aluminium bonnets and wings to save weight, and an aluminium panel is a different repair — different filler, different preparation, and it has to be isolated from steel tooling and steel dust, because steel particles left in aluminium start a corrosion reaction that shows up as blistering months after the car left the workshop. There is no surcharge on our rate card for it. There is a difference in how it is handled.

Collision figures are DfT STATS19 road casualty data as presented by crashview.co.uk, counted by postcode district over the full recorded period. Parking figures are the London Borough of Sutton’s own ward parking surveys.

Your questions answered

Do you do paintless dent removal?

No. The panel is straightened and then painted and blended. On a shallow dent with unbroken paint a PDR specialist will be cheaper than us; on a crease, a dent near an edge, or anything with the paint broken, PDR cannot do it at all. We would rather tell you that now than after your car is here.

The bonnet is aluminium. Does that cost more?

Not on our rate card. It is handled differently - different filler, different preparation, and isolated from steel tooling, because steel particles left in aluminium start a corrosion reaction that blisters months later. The difference is in the method, not the price.

How long will my car be with you, coming from Wimbledon?

A single panel is two to three days, because the paint has to cure before it is polished and refitted. At 20 minutes away it is worth asking about collection when you book, rather than making the trip twice.
