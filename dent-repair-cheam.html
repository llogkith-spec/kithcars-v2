Dents in Cheam, and where they come from

Cheam runs the opposite way to what you would guess, and the council’s own figures show it.

SM3 has 1,850 recorded collisions. Across the London Borough of Sutton the total is 11,569, with 1,588 of them serious.

Cheam households own more cars than Carshalton ones - 61 per cent have two or more against 41 per cent - and yet only 34 per cent park on the street against 56 per cent in Carshalton. Cheam has driveways. That changes the damage completely: far less door-to-door contact and fewer clipped mirrors, and instead gatepost scrapes on the nearside and reversing damage on drives that were laid out for one car in 1935 and now hold two.

What the council’s parking survey found in Cheam. 44 per cent of residents said their street has a parking problem, 61 per cent of households run two or more cars and 34 per cent park at least one on the road. The streets that responded most were Matlock Crescent, Burdon Lane, Kingsdown Road and Tilehurst Road.

Driveway damage also tends to arrive later than street damage, because you see it every day and it stops registering. By the time a Cheam car comes in, the gatepost scrape down the nearside has often been there a year and has begun to lift at the edges. That is still repairable, but the preparation is longer: anything lifting has to come back to sound paint before anything new goes on, or the new edge lifts in the same place within a season.

Collision figures are DfT STATS19 road casualty data as presented by crashview.co.uk, counted by postcode district over the full recorded period. Parking figures are the London Borough of Sutton’s own ward parking surveys.

Your questions answered

Do you do paintless dent removal?

No. The panel is straightened and then painted and blended. On a shallow dent with unbroken paint a PDR specialist will be cheaper than us; on a crease, a dent near an edge, or anything with the paint broken, PDR cannot do it at all. We would rather tell you that now than after your car is here.

The scrape has been on the car for a year. Is it too late?

Not too late, but it is more work than it was. Paint that has been open that long usually lifts at the edges, and anything lifting has to come back to sound paint first or the new edge lifts in the same place within a season.

How long will my car be with you, coming from Cheam?

A single panel is two to three days, because the paint has to cure before it is polished and refitted. At 12 minutes away you can drop it and collect it easily enough.
