Norbury drivers

What we see on cars from Norbury

Life on the A23 shapes what wears out on a Norbury car. London Road is a red route that shuffles forward between bus stops and signals, and the queues thicken towards Thornton Heath Pond and the Lombard roundabout, so a short journey can mean working the clutch over and over between stops. In a manual that shows up as a biting point creeping higher, a shudder as you pull away, or the rattle at idle that often points to a tired dual mass flywheel. Automatics suffer differently: torque converters slip and generate heat when the car is repeatedly crawling, and on gearboxes with a service interval we would rather change the fluid than wait for harsh shifts to appear.

Slow traffic does not mean nothing happens to the paintwork. Most of the accident work we take in from SW16 is low speed and unglamorous, rear bumpers and tailgates nudged in queueing traffic, wings caught pulling out of a side turning, wing mirrors taken off on the narrow, parked stretches around Northborough Road and the cottage estate. We handle both insurance and self-pay repairs, and where a car is older we will say honestly whether a smart repair and a blend is the better call than a new panel, because matching faded paint on a twelve-year-old car is its own skill.

The low ground matters too. London Road crosses Norbury Brook at Hermitage Bridge, and the brook can rise quickly after heavy rain, so cars here see standing water more often than most. Water and road salt find the same places every time: brake pipes, sills, suspension mounts and subframe seams. We look hard at all of it at MOT time, and where corrosion has gone past a wire-brush job we can weld and repair rather than write the car off. Anything found is quoted before we touch it.

Questions

Norbury: your questions answered

Is it worth driving five and a half miles for a repair?

Five and a half miles is a trip most people would rather make once, so collection and delivery is there if you want it and otherwise the car is best left with us for the day. Because the bodyshop and the workshop are on one site, a job that would otherwise mean two garages and two journeys becomes a single drop-off.

Norbury is mostly in Croydon but partly in Lambeth. Does that matter to you?

Not at all, we cover the whole of SW16 either way. Most of Norbury sits in Croydon, with some streets on the Streatham side falling under Lambeth, and that boundary decides your parking permit and street services rather than anything to do with the repair. Give us the full address when you book or when we collect and that is all we need.

Will driving to you cost me anything extra in ULEZ?

No. Norbury and Hackbridge are both within the London-wide ULEZ, in force across all London boroughs since August 2023, so there is no additional zone between us. If your car is already charged daily at home, driving here changes nothing.

My car has been through standing water near the brook. What should I do?

If the engine stopped in the water, do not try to restart it, as drawing water into the cylinders causes serious damage. Call us and we can recover it instead. If it drove out normally, we would still check the oil for contamination, along with the brakes, wheel bearings and underbody electrics.
