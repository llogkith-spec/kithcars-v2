Town centre parking damage dominates: the St Nicholas and Times Square multi-storeys come up by name more than anywhere else. Tight bays, concrete pillars and ramps that catch a lower bumper edge. The upside is that almost all of it happens slowly, so it is paint and panel rather than structure - which is the cheaper end of our rate card and often a same-week job.

Sutton is also the town where we are most often asked whether it is worth claiming, and the answer is usually no. Multi-storey damage is nearly always a single panel, and a single panel on our rate card frequently lands below a typical total excess once the compulsory and voluntary halves are added together. Claiming for it costs you most of the bill anyway, loses some no-claims discount, and gets declared for about five years. Get a real figure first and then compare — it is a two-minute sum that saves people a lot.

Collision figures are DfT STATS19 road casualty data as presented by crashview.co.uk, counted by postcode district over the full recorded period. Parking figures are the London Borough of Sutton’s own ward parking surveys.

How we do it

We straighten the panel and then paint it, blending into the panels either side. We do not do paintless dent removal — on a shallow dent with unbroken paint a PDR specialist will be cheaper, and on a crease, a dent near an edge or anything with the paint split, PDR cannot do it at all. Your questions answered

Do you do paintless dent removal?

No. The panel is straightened and then painted and blended. On a shallow dent with unbroken paint a PDR specialist will be cheaper than us; on a crease, a dent near an edge, or anything with the paint broken, PDR cannot do it at all. We would rather tell you that now than after your car is here.

It happened in the multi-storey. Does that make it a claim?

Rarely worth it. Car park damage is almost always a single panel, and a single panel often lands below a typical total excess once the compulsory and voluntary halves are added together. Two-minute sum, and it usually says pay directly.

How long will my car be with you, coming from Sutton?

A single panel is two to three days, because the paint has to cure before it is polished and refitted. At 10 minutes away you can drop it and collect it easily enough.
