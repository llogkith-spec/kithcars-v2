Croydon drivers

What we see on cars from Croydon

Town-centre Croydon is unusually hard on paint and panels. Multi-lane gyratories, the Flyover and Underpass, tram tracks running on-street and tightly parked 20mph side streets mean a lot of close-quarters manoeuvring, and the damage that follows is rarely dramatic: a mirror taken off in a narrow gap, a bumper corner cracked reversing into a post, a rear quarter creased by someone else's door. A dent is straightened and the panel repainted in the car's own colour, blended into the panels either side so it does not show on a car you may sell. Where it is broken, the panel is prepared, primed, sprayed and lacquered so that the repaired area matches in daylight rather than only in the workshop.

Mechanically, the borough's roads produce a very particular pattern of wear. Fiveways, where Purley Way meets the A232 near Waddon, is one of the busiest junctions around, the Lombard and Thornton Heath Pond roundabouts queue at peak times, and the A236 Mitcham Road crawls on the approach to the centre. Constant stop-start in traffic loads the front brakes and the clutch, while suspension bushes and dampers take a beating from repeated low-speed direction changes on 20mph streets. Tram rails crossing the road are another quiet hazard: hitting them at an angle at speed is a good way to kerb an alloy or split a tyre sidewall, which is not always obvious until the tyre goes down days later.

The vehicle mix here is as broad as the borough. Private-hire and delivery cars working the town centre and Purley Way, family cars in Addiscombe and Shirley, tradespeople's vans out of the Purley Way and Beddington Lane estates, and plenty of older cars being kept on the road because the whole borough has been inside the ULEZ since August 2023 and replacing a compliant car is expensive. Camera-enforced Healthy Neighbourhood filters and red route rules on the A23 and A232 add another reason to plan a route rather than improvise one. For cars being kept long-term, the work that matters is unglamorous: fluids changed on time, corrosion caught early and suspension replaced before it wrecks tyres.

The route

From Croydon to Soho Mills

From the town centre take the Croydon Flyover, the A232, west onto Duppas Hill Road and Epsom Road, down to Fiveways where it meets Purley Way. Carry straight on along Croydon Road through Waddon and Beddington, past Beddington Park, to the Wallington junction with Manor Road and London Road. Turn right onto London Road, the A237, and head north into Hackbridge, where Soho Mills is on the right. About 4.5 miles, roughly fifteen minutes when Fiveways is moving.

From the town centre take the Croydon Flyover (A232) west onto Duppas Hill Road and Epsom Road to the Fiveways junction with Purley Way (A23). Continue on Croydon Road (A232) through Waddon and Beddington, past Beddington Park, to the junction with Manor Road and London Road in Wallington, then turn right onto London Road (A237) and head north to Hackbridge; Soho Mills is on the right.

Questions

Croydon: your questions answered

Can you handle an insurance claim, or is it self-pay only?

We take on both insurance and self-pay accident repair. For smaller damage it is worth getting our written quote first and comparing it with your excess, because paying directly and protecting a no-claims discount often works out better on a single scuffed panel or bumper.

My car is not driveable after a knock in the town centre. Can you collect it?

Ring 020 4629 9469, tell us exactly where the car is and what has happened, and we will confirm whether we can recover it and when. Access is the thing that decides it, so say if the car is in a multi-storey, on a red route or somewhere with a height restriction. We run the recovery ourselves, so the car comes straight to the people who will repair it.

Does driving out to Hackbridge take me through any charges or filters?

The whole of Croydon and all of our side of the boundary are inside the London-wide ULEZ, so there is no zone boundary between you and us. The borough also runs camera-enforced Healthy Neighbourhood filters on certain residential streets, so if you intend to cut through side roads rather than follow the A232 out through Waddon, check the council's current map before you set off.

I drive private hire. How quickly can you turn work around?

Tell us at the point of booking that the car is your income and we will plan around it, which usually means an early drop-off, parts ordered before the car arrives and, where possible, the work finished the same day. Some jobs such as paint genuinely cannot be rushed, and we will say so up front rather than after we have the keys.
