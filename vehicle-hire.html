How we handle vehicle hire at Hackbridge

A hire car is only ever as good as its last handover, so every vehicle goes back on the ramp between hires instead of straight back out. Oil and coolant levels, pad thickness measured rather than eyeballed, tread depth and pressures, lights, wipers and washers, and a fault code scan for anything the previous driver did not think to mention. Whatever turns up is put right here before the car is offered again, which is the practical advantage of a small hire fleet living inside a working garage.

Servicing follows the manufacturer's schedule rather than a fleet's idea of one, and the cars are MOT'd here too. Tyres are changed well before the legal limit, because a hire car may be driven on a wet motorway by somebody who has never sat in it before. Between hires the interior is cleaned, pressures are reset, and any mark picked up on the last hire is dealt with rather than simply added to a list of existing damage.

The terms are explained before you sign, and we would rather talk you out of hiring than have you discover something awkward at the counter. Age and licence requirements, the deposit, what the insurance covers and what the excess would be, who else may drive, and the mileage arrangement are all set out first. Charging zones deserve a specific mention in this part of London: any ULEZ, Congestion Charge or Dartford crossing charge run up during your hire is yours to settle, so check the route before you set off. If the repair overruns, one phone call extends the hire.

The cars we see

The fleet is made up of ordinary cars rather than anything exotic: small hatchbacks and family-sized vehicles that suit a commute, a school run and a supermarket boot. Manuals are the easiest to promise and automatics are limited, so if an automatic is the only thing you can drive, say so at the point of booking. The same goes for child seats and ISOFIX points, or for anything bigger than a five-seater. Charging zone status varies from vehicle to vehicle, so ask if your route takes you into central London.

Worth knowing

What people get wrong about vehicle hire

My own insurance will cover it

Policies that let you drive another car usually do so third party only, and many exclude vehicles hired to you outright. Read the wording, or ask us what the hire itself includes.

A ULEZ car pays no charges

The ULEZ and the Congestion Charge are separate schemes with separate rules. A fully compliant car still pays the Congestion Charge inside the zone during its hours of operation.

A friend can drive it too

Only drivers named on the agreement are covered. Handing the keys to somebody who is not on it leaves you both exposed and can invalidate the cover completely.

Fines follow the driver home

Speeding, parking and charging penalties issued during a hire are traced to the vehicle and passed on to whoever had it. They do not quietly disappear when the car comes back.

Questions

Vehicle Hire: your questions answered

Can I hire a car if you are not repairing mine?

Ask us. The vehicles exist first for customers whose cars are with us, so anything else depends on what is free across the dates you want. Ring with your dates rather than assuming it either way.

Who else is allowed to drive it?

Only drivers named on the agreement, and each of them has to meet the same licence and age requirements you do. If your partner will be doing the school run in it, bring their licence along at the same time and we will add them properly.

What happens if I damage it?

Tell us straight away rather than at the end of the hire. The condition agreed at the start is what any damage is judged against, and the deposit and excess arrangement is set out before you sign so you already know where you stand.

Can you bring the car to me?

We collect and deliver locally, so in many cases the hire car arrives as your own is picked up. It depends on the distance and how the day is running, so agree it when you book rather than on the morning.

What do I need to hire a vehicle?

Typically a full valid driving licence, proof of identity and address, a deposit and suitable insurance cover. There may also be minimum age and licence-held requirements. Ask us for the exact terms when you book, as they can vary by vehicle.

Is a courtesy car the same as hire?

No. Courtesy cars are offered subject to availability. Hire is a separate daily-rate service, useful for longer repairs or when no courtesy car is free. We will tell you which options are available for your job.

Is insurance included?

Ask us when you book. Depending on the arrangement, cover may be provided with the hire or you may need your own, and there is typically an excess or deposit. We explain the terms before you sign anything.

Can I hire from you after an accident?

If your insurer, or the other driver's insurer, provides a replacement vehicle, that is usually the first option to check. If you are paying yourself or cover is not available, you can hire from us while your car is repaired at our bodyshop.

What if my repair takes longer than planned?

We call you as soon as we know, and you can extend the hire. You pay for the days you have the vehicle, so we aim to give accurate timescales from the start.
