Vehicle Hire for Hackbridge drivers

Based on London Road, not dispatched to it

Our yard is on London Road in Hackbridge, which puts us a couple of minutes from the station, the parade and the level crossing, and a short run from the A237 and the A217 beyond it.

That matters more for recovery than for anything else we do, because recovery is the one service where the distance to you is the service. We are not a call centre passing your job to whoever is nearest and taking a cut. If you have broken down in Hackbridge, Wallington, Hackbridge or Mitcham, the truck coming to you is ours and it is starting from here.

It also means a car can come straight back to our own workshop rather than to a compound to wait. If the fault is something we can put right, it is already in the right place and nobody is paying twice to move it.

And if you are local and the car will not start, it is worth a phone call before a recovery at all. A fair number of the jobs we get called out for around here are a flat battery on a car that only ever does the station run, which is a jump and a conversation rather than a tow.

What to do while you wait

  • Get everyone somewhere safe. Out of the car and off the carriageway if you are on a fast road, behind a barrier if there is one.
  • Hazards on, and a warning triangle if it is safe to place one. Not on a motorway.
  • Tell us what the car is doing. Whether it rolls, steers and has a working handbrake decides which truck comes, and getting that right first time saves an hour.
  • Say if it is electric or hybrid. A damaged high-voltage car has its own handling requirements and we would rather know before we arrive.
  • Check it is not just the battery. Around here it often is.

We will give you a real time rather than a window, and the car can come straight back to our own workshop rather than sitting in a compound.

Vehicle Hire: what’s involved

Some repairs are quick, but bodywork, engine or gearbox work, or waiting for parts, can keep a car off the road for several days. We offer courtesy cars subject to availability, and where one is not available or you need a vehicle for longer, you can hire one from us on a daily basis while we work on yours.

Hiring from the garage doing the repair keeps things simple: you drop your car off and drive away in the same visit, and we match the hire period to the repair timescale. As with any hire, you will typically need a valid driving licence, a deposit and suitable insurance cover, and there may be age or licence-history requirements. When to arrange hire

It is best to ask about hire when you book the repair or as soon as we give you the quote, so we can check availability and match the dates. For planned work, a day or two's notice helps. If your car has been recovered to us after a breakdown or accident, we can talk through options when we call with the diagnosis. Hire is charged per day, so we give you a realistic repair timescale up front.

  • Ask at the same time as booking your repair where possible
  • Charged per day from collection to return
  • Call us to extend if the repair takes longer than planned
  • For accident claims, check your insurer's replacement vehicle cover first

How hire works

  1. Ask when you bookTell us the dates you need and the work being done on your car, and we check what is available.
  2. Confirm the requirementsWe explain what is needed, which typically includes a valid driving licence, proof of identity and address, a deposit and insurance cover. Ask us for the exact terms.
  3. Condition checkBefore you drive away, we record the hire vehicle's condition, mileage and fuel level with you.
  4. Drive awayYou leave your car with us and take the hire vehicle in the same visit.
  5. Stay in touchWe update you on your repair, and if it runs over we agree an extension with you.
  6. ReturnBring the vehicle back with the same fuel level. We check it over with you, settle the charge and hand back your car.
