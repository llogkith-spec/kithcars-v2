Dent Removal: what’s involved

Paintless dent repair (PDR) works the metal back into shape from behind the panel using specialist rods, or pulls it from the front with glue tabs where there's no access. Because the panel isn't filled or painted, the factory finish is kept and there's no colour matching involved. It works well on door dings, car park dents, hail damage and soft dents on flat or gently curved panels.

PDR has limits. If the paint is cracked or chipped, the dent has a sharp crease, the metal has stretched, or the dent sits right on a panel edge or swage line, PDR alone usually won't give a proper result. Aluminium panels are also harder to work. In those cases we repair traditionally: the metal is pulled and shaped, a thin skim of filler is applied, then the area is primed and painted, colour matched and blended into the surrounding panels.

Types of dent and what they need

Door dings

Small round dents from car park doors. With the paint intact these are usually ideal for PDR.

Hail damage

Many shallow dents across the roof, bonnet and boot. Usually suitable for PDR if the paint hasn't cracked, though it takes time.

Soft, shallow dents

Dents from leaning, a ball or a trolley where the metal hasn't stretched can often be removed completely by PDR.

Sharp creases

Folded metal can often be improved by PDR, but for an invisible result it usually needs filler and paint.

Dents with cracked or chipped paint

These need traditional repair and refinishing, and should be done promptly because the exposed metal will rust.

Dents on swage lines or panel edges

The metal is stiffer or double-skinned there and access is harder, so results by PDR vary.

Dents in plastic bumpers

PDR is for metal panels. Plastic can often be reshaped with controlled heat instead, as part of a bumper repair.

When should it be done?

Sooner is better. A dent with intact paint won't get worse on its own, but paint on a stretched area can crack over time, and once moisture gets under it, PDR is no longer an option. Any dent where the paint is already chipped or cracked should be repaired promptly to stop rust. Hail damage is often covered by comprehensive insurance, so check your policy before paying privately. Removing dents before selling or returning a lease car can cost less than the deduction.

  • Dents with intact paint: any time, but before the paint has a chance to crack
  • Dents with cracked or chipped paint: as soon as practical to prevent corrosion
  • After a hailstorm: once your insurer has seen the damage, if you're claiming
  • Before a sale or lease return inspection

How we remove a dent

  1. Assessment under lightingWe examine the dent under a reflection board or line light to see its true shape and depth, check the paint, and tell you whether PDR or traditional repair is right before quoting.
  2. Gain accessTrim, lights or inner panels are removed so tools can reach behind the dent. Where there's no access, we use glue-pull tabs from the front.
  3. Work the dent outThe metal is moved back with many small, controlled pushes, working from the outside of the dent inwards, and high spots are gently knocked down.
  4. Check and refineThe panel is checked repeatedly under the reflection board until the reflection runs straight with no highs or lows.
  5. Traditional repair where neededIf PDR isn't suitable, the metal is pulled and shaped as close as possible, then finished with a thin layer of fine filler and primer.
  6. Paint where neededColour matched from your paint code, blended into the surrounding panels and finished with clear coat and controlled curing.
  7. Refit and hand overTrim and fittings are refitted, windows and locks checked, and we inspect the result with you.

Tools and materials

PDR needs no parts or paint. The work is done with specialist rods, glue tabs and pulling tools, and checked under a line board or reflector light. Where traditional repair is needed, we use fine polyester filler applied thinly over properly shaped metal, never as a substitute for shaping it, followed by primer, basecoat mixed to your paint code and a two-pack clear coat.

  • PDR rods and hand tools for working behind the panel
  • Glue-pull tabs for areas without rear access
  • Reflection board or line light for checking the panel
  • Fine filler, primer and paint mixed to your paint code for traditional repairs
  • New trim clips where panels are removed for access

If it’s left

A dent with undamaged paint is mostly cosmetic and won't spread, though the paint in a stretched area can crack later. Dent Removal in Wimbledon: your questions answered

Can you remove dents from an electric car?

Yes. PDR works the same way on an EV's body panels, though many use aluminium, which needs more care. We don't need to touch the high-voltage system for panel work, and we check any sensors or wiring near the trim we remove.

Is it worth coming to Hackbridge during the Championships?

Local traffic is much heavier then, with road closures near the All England Club and bus diversions. If you can, book before or after the fortnight, or ask about collection so you don't have to drive through it.

Will my parking sensors still work after a dent repair?

They should. Most dent work on doors and wings doesn't involve sensors, but where trim or panels near them are removed, we refit everything carefully and check the sensors and cameras before handing the car back.

Can every dent be removed without painting?

No. PDR needs intact paint and metal that hasn't been stretched too far. Sharp creases, cracked paint, dents right on a panel edge and some aluminium panels usually need traditional repair. We'll tell you which applies when we inspect the dent.

Will the dent be completely invisible?

Most small, soft dents can be made practically invisible. Deep or creased dents can be improved a great deal by PDR but may leave a faint mark visible in certain light. We tell you what to expect beforehand and offer the traditional route if you want a perfect finish.

Can you remove hail damage?

Yes, usually by PDR as long as the paint hasn't cracked. A hail-damaged car can have dozens of dents, so it takes longer than a single ding. Hail damage is often covered by comprehensive insurance.

Can PDR damage the paint?

Done carefully, no. Factory paint is flexible enough to move with the metal. Panels that have been repainted or filled before are less predictable and can crack, so we check for signs of previous repair and warn you of any risk first.

Can you remove dents from plastic bumpers?

Not with PDR, which is for metal panels. Plastic bumpers can often be reshaped with controlled heat, and refinished if the paint is damaged, as part of a bumper repair.
