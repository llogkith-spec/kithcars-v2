Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

This policy covers KITH Cars Garage and KITH Bodyworks & Accident Repair Centre, trading names of Kith Cars Ltd (company number 15013598, VAT 470844577), Unit 1, Soho Mills, London Road, Hackbridge, SM6 7HN. Kith Cars Ltd is the data controller for the personal information described here.

Questions about your information: email kithcarsinfo@gmail.com, call 020 4629 9469, or write to the address above.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and, where needed, your address (for example for collection, delivery or recovery).
  • Vehicle details: registration, make, model and year, mileage, and what the car needs. When you enter a registration we look up the vehicle’s details from the DVLA and MOT records.
  • Your messages: what you send us by our website forms, WhatsApp, text, phone, email, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or Google, and our replies. Photos and videos you send of your vehicle or damage.
  • Job records: quotes, bookings, inspection and health-check results, photos and videos of your vehicle while it is with us, work carried out, invoices and payments.
  • Website use: which pages are visited and which buttons are clicked. See “Cookies and analytics” below.

We do not need, and do not ask for, any sensitive information such as health details. We do not keep card numbers; card payments are handled by the payment provider.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, give you a price range and a quote, and book your vehicle in.
  • To carry out the work, keep you updated while your car is with us, and invoice you.
  • To send booking reminders, follow up on a quote you have not answered, and after collection ask for a review or offer a referral link.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations, and to handle any warranty claim or complaint.
  • To understand which parts of our website bring in work, so we can improve it.

Our legal bases under UK GDPR are: taking steps you ask for before a contract and performing it (quotes, bookings, the work itself); our legal obligations (tax and accounting records); and our legitimate interests in running and improving the business (follow-ups, reviews, website analytics). Where we rely on your consent, for example for marketing messages, you can withdraw it at any time.

Automated replies and pricing

When you contact us, our system may read your message to send it to the right member of the team and send you an automatic acknowledgement. For some mechanical enquiries it also sends a price range, worked out from your vehicle, the work described and our price list. The range is a guide only: a member of our team checks every job before you receive an exact quote, and nothing is booked or charged without your agreement. If you would like a person to deal with your enquiry from the start, just say so.

WhatsApp and other messaging

If you message us on WhatsApp, or ask us to contact you there, we use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to send and receive those messages, including quotes, booking confirmations and reminders. We only message you about your enquiry or job. Reply STOP at any time and we will stop sending you automatic messages.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Messaging and social platforms you contact us on (Meta for WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook; Google; TikTok).
  • Our hosting, database and email providers (including Netlify, Supabase and Google), which store our systems and records.
  • AI and software providers that power our message sorting and pricing tools.
  • Parts suppliers and subcontractors (for example MOT testing stations) when needed to complete your job.
  • Payment and finance providers such as Payment Assist, if you choose to use them.
  • Your insurer or claims handler, if your repair is an insurance or non-fault claim and you ask us to deal with them.
  • Our accountant, and HMRC or other authorities where the law requires.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, for example in the US or the EU. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK-US data bridge.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure visits and how many people request a quote, and to measure our adverts. These can set cookies in your browser. The events we send do not include your name, number or email. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on.

How long we keep it

  • Invoices and job records: 6 years after the end of the tax year, as UK tax law requires.
  • Enquiries and quotes that did not go ahead: up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Message history: for as long as you are a customer, and up to 2 years after your last contact.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another provider. You can object to follow-up and marketing messages at any time. Email kithcarsinfo@gmail.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
