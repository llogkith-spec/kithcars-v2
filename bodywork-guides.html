Your questions answered

Do I have to use my insurer’s repairer?

No. The repairer is your choice on any claim. We are on no insurer panel and no manufacturer scheme, so nothing here is steered by an arrangement.

Can I get a price from these pages?

The panel guides carry the rate card, and the box on every page gives you a band from your registration. These pages explain the why rather than the how much.

Do you do paintless dent removal?

No. Every dent we repair is straightened and then painted and blended. On the dents PDR can fix, a PDR specialist will be cheaper, and we would rather tell you.

Something here is out of date.

Tell us and we will fix it. The figures on these pages come from published sources and our own rate card, and both move.
